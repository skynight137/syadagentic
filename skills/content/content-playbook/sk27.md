## 1. Content strategy builder

Before producing, settle the foundation (once, store it in the sk15 vault, reuse):

```
PILLARS    → 3-5 core themes (e.g. education · behind-scenes · proof · opinion · CTA). Ratio 70-20-10.
AUDIENCE   → who, what problem, on which platform, in what language
POSITIONING→ 1 sentence: "for [audience] who [problem], I [unique solution]"
TONE       → take it from brand voice (sk20) / USER.md
GOAL        → awareness | trust | leads | sales — different goal, different format
```

Output = a "content OS" that drives every later decision. Without it, content turns random.

## 3. Platform-specific adapter

One message, different clothes per platform — do NOT copy-paste identically:

| Platform | Winning format | Length | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X/Twitter** | thread, punchy one-liner | short, hook in tweet 1 | fast, opinion |
| **LinkedIn** | thought leadership, story | medium, short lines | professional-personal |
| **Instagram** | carousel, visual-first | medium caption + hook | visual, relatable |
| **TikTok/Reels** | short video script | 15-60s, hook in first 3 seconds | energetic, native |


## 4. Viral hook & scroll-stopper

The hook is 80% of performance. Stop the scroll in 3 seconds / the first line:

```
HOOK PATTERNS:
- Contrast   : "Everyone says X. They're wrong."
- Numbers     : "3 mistakes that cost you..."
- Question : "Why does nobody see your posts?"
- Stakes     : "I lost $5k before I realised this"
- Curiosity gap: "Here's what nobody tells you about..."
```

The hook must be relevant to the body (clickbait that doesn't deliver kills trust). Clear CTA at the end: one action, not five.
